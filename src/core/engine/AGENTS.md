# engine

**Parent context:** `../AGENTS.md`

## Purpose
Turns a `ParamState` plus MIDI events into stereo audio. `Params` is the single source of truth for every parameter. `Voice` renders one note at the oversampled rate. `Engine` handles polyphony/mono/legato, global LFOs, parameter smoothing, the mod matrix, decimation and the FX chain.

## For AI Agents

### Working In This Directory
- **Adding a parameter:** add one `X(...)` line to `PSY_PARAMS` in `Params.h`. The enum, JUCE layout, state save and generator ranges pick it up automatically. Then read it in `Voice.cpp`/`Engine.cpp`, add a widget (`src/ui/SynthPanels.cpp`, `FxPage.cpp`, `KickPanels.cpp`, `PhosPanels.cpp` or `Pages.cpp`, whichever page the parameter lives on), and optionally set it in recipes.
- **Parameter ids (`"reverb_mix"`) are the save format.** Presets and host state store values by id. Renaming an id silently drops saved values.
- **Order matters for repeated blocks.** `modSrcParam(slot)` assumes Src/Dst/Amt triples starting at `Mod1Src` (`PSY_MOD_SLOT`), and `lfoParam(lfo, off)` assumes 6 params per LFO from `Lfo1Shape`. Insert new params outside those blocks.
- Adding a choice → update the matching `choices::` vector in `Params.cpp`. Mod sources/destinations must match the `ModSrc`/`ModDst` enums, and the `PSY_MOD_SLOT` maxima (`Mod*Src` 18, `Mod*Dst` 58 today) must be `Count - 1` (`[params]` checks). Append new sources/targets at the end: saved patches store their index.
- **`ModDst` layout** (indices are saved, so never insert or reorder): the synth voice targets `None`..`FilterEnvAmt`; `F2Drive`, `F2EnvAmt` (voice targets that were appended later and sit before the effects); the effects `DistDrive`..`WidthAmount`; the Phoscyon voice `PhosCutoff`..`PhosTune`; the Phoscyon effects `PhosDistDrive`..`PhosReverbMix`; `Count`. `isFxTarget` is true for `DistDrive`..`WidthAmount` **and for everything from `PhosDistDrive` up to (not including) `Count`**; `isPhosVoiceTarget` is `PhosCutoff`..`PhosTune`. `prepareContext` sends effect targets to `fxSlots` (summed by `modulateFx`, which spans `DistDrive`..`Count` and skips what `isFxTarget` rejects) and everything else to the voice slots (`ctx.slots`).
- **Adding a mod target:** append to `ModDst` (after `PhosReverbMix`) and `choices::modDst`, raise the `PSY_MOD_SLOT` `Dst` maximum, and give it a `kTargets` row with its knob (the knob then takes drops and draws rings; effect targets need it, since `modulateFx` moves the knob's normalised value). What else depends on the kind:
  - a **new effect target** is already inside `isFxTarget`'s open-ended upper range, so `modulateFx` picks it up; the effect just has to read its parameter from the `fxParams` it is given.
  - a **new synth voice target** appended at the end would also land in that range and be treated as an effect. Close the range first: make `isFxTarget` end at `PhosReverbMix`, then read the new target in `Voice::render` (`M (ModDst::…)`).
  - a **new Phoscyon voice target** needs the same range fix, a `case` in `Engine::phosMods` and a field in `PhosMods` that `PhosVoice` reads.
  - **LFO Rate targets** work on a voice's own LFO (any source) and on a free LFO (global sources only; `prepareContext` → `freeLfoHz`).
- `Engine::process` and `Voice::render` are audio-thread code. Keep them `noexcept`, with no allocation.
- Quality (1x/2x/4x; `RenderQuality` when offline) changes `osFactor` and latency, after the sounding voices have faded out (see `followQuality`). The plugin polls `latencySamples()` every block.
- **Phoscyon mode.** `voiceNoteOn` sends notes to the single `PhosVoice` (`phosNoteOn`: legato notes slide). Its mod targets are `ModDst::PhosCutoff`..`PhosTune` (voice, offsets on the knob in `phosMods`) and `PhosDistDrive`..`PhosReverbMix` (effects, `isFxTarget`). `processFx` plays `phosFxChain(FxOrder, PhosEqSlot, PhosLimSlot)`: the nine shared slots with Phoscyon's distortion/chorus/delay/reverb in place of ours, plus EQ and limiter; the limiter has 3.3 ms of lookahead. The arp plays non-accented steps at `plainVelocity` 0.4 so only accented steps cross the accent threshold.
- **Kick mode.** `voiceNoteOn` sends notes to the two `kicks` when `EngineMode` is Kick. Note-ons within 1 ms of a fresh hit merge into it, and note-off is ignored. `followEngineMode()` releases the synth voices and chokes the kicks when the mode changes. `ArpSettings::from` clamps the gate below 1 and drops slides in kick mode, because a tie would swallow a kick. After `reset()`, `process()` sets `cur = target` before handling any event, so re-sent notes play the new patch.

### Testing Requirements
- `psybox_tests "[engine]"`: init patch sound/silence, every category renders finite non-silent audio (energy sounds hold one note), all quality modes match, parameter fuzzing. `"[params]"` checks mapping round-trips and the matrix lists. `"[energy]"`: the sweep lasts its bars at the tempo (or its seconds), the fade follows it. `"[fx]"`: effects follow their matrix modulation. `"[blocks]"`: static patches render the same bits at any block size, modulated ones (LFOs, envelopes into effects, the 303's LFO) within -60 dB when the chunks line up, events at block edges. `"[click]"` also covers Volume/Level moves, quality changes, the unison hand-over, the 303's Amp modulation and the mod wheel. `"[stress]"` reads `Engine::diagnostics()`: no NaN or infinity anywhere, pre-clip peak under +24 dBFS, and a voice poisoned on purpose is replaced. `"[rates]"`: silence at rest and 303/kick tuning at 44.1/48/96 kHz and all qualities.
- `psybox_tests "[phoscyon]"`: tuning, slide, accent, cutoff/envmod/decay laws, generated patches, arp accents, distortion models bounded, chain slots, EQ and limiter, mode switching, fuzzing.
- `psybox_tests "[kick]"`: identical hits, body on the played key, octave independence, the sweep landing, the punch envelope, one-shot, click-free retrigger, chord merge, mode switching, quality independence, no arp ties, fuzzing.

### Common Patterns
- Plain (denormalised) values live in `ParamState::v`. Use `get`, `geti`, `on`. `set` snaps discrete params.
- The generator works in normalised space via `ParamDef::toNorm/toPlain`.
